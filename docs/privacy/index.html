MuZip Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy covers MuZip, a text message service operated by Jacob Ouyang, a sole proprietor, operating as MuZip.

What is collected

  • Your phone number.
  • The messages you send to the MuZip number: your questions, and the small coded pictures and close-ups the MuZip app makes from your photos. Your full photo stays on your phone and is never sent.
  • MuZip's replies to you.

How it is used

Only to answer your questions. To do that, your question and the coded pictures are sent to Meta's AI model service, which works out the answer. Messages travel through Twilio, the text message provider. Messages are kept on the operator's computer so a conversation can continue; email muzip2026@gmail.com to have yours deleted.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your phone number and messages are shared only with the service providers named above, and only to deliver and answer your messages.

Stopping

Reply STOP to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. See the MuZip SMS Terms.

Contact

Email muzip2026@gmail.com.
