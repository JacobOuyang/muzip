MuZip SMS Terms

Last updated November 11, 2026

The program

MuZip is a text message service operated by Jacob Ouyang, a sole proprietor, operating as MuZip. It is invite-only: people the operator has invited text the MuZip number a question together with a small coded picture made by the MuZip app on their own phone. MuZip replies by text, sometimes first asking for a closer look at part of the picture (a "ZOOM" line the app answers automatically), and then with an answer.

How often you get messages

MuZip never texts first. It only replies to messages you send, usually 1 to 5 messages per question you ask.

Message and data rates may apply.

Help and stopping

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages; you will get one message confirming you have been unsubscribed and nothing more after that. Reply START to sign up again.
